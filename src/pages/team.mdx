## Contribute

We're always open to contributors. If you want to write a guide or article for TechHut, just submit a pull request with your content. Check out our [contributor docs](https://github.com/TechHutTV/techhut.tv/blob/main/docs/README.md) to get started. If it's your first post, you'll need to add yourself as an author -- your profile photo must be a real image of yourself, not a drawing or avatar.
