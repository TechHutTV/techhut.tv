TechHut is a small operation built around actually testing the stuff we cover. The videos and guides are based on real deployments, which means spinning up clean systems, following every step from beginning to end, and finding the weird permissions issue or outdated command before somebody else gets stuck on it.

I'm looking for people who already run this technology at home, care about the details, and can explain what they learned without making it sound more complicated than it is. The goal is pretty simple: figure out what actually works, document it clearly, and give the community something they can follow without having to guess their way through the missing steps.
